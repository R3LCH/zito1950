# ZITO1950 · Design system (binding)

Tokens: `design/zito.tokens.json` (mirrored in `src/styles/index.css` `@theme`). Components: `design/components.json`. Background research: `research/design-direction.md`.

## Atmosphere
Quiet Italian family catalogue on paper-white. Editorial, product-led, sober. Luxury comes from real photography, rhythm and hierarchy, never from effects. Variance 5 · motion 3 · density 2.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| bg | #FFFFFF | Every surface, footer included. No dark sections, no colored washes. |
| ink | #141414 | Titles, body, controls, focus ring, logo. |
| ink-2 | #3A3A3A | Secondary prose. |
| muted | #6B6B6B | Captions/meta only (5.33:1 on white, AA). |
| line | #E7E7E5 | Hairline dividers (1px). |
| well | #F5F5F3 | Image wells behind isolated products. |
| gold | #7A5C14 | «1 of 1» label text and border only (5.78:1 on gold-wash). |
| gold-wash | #FBF6E9 | «1 of 1» label ground only. |
No other accent hue in the page UI; gold is flat (no gradient, no metallic effect) and never used beyond the «1 of 1» label. Footer social logos are the exception: Instagram #E4405F, Facebook #1877F2, WhatsApp #25D366, retained on hover.

## Typography
- Serif `EB Garamond` 400/500 (italic only when meaningful) for headings, model names, quotes.
- Sans `Manrope Variable` 400/500/600 for body, nav, controls.
- Brand inscriptions `ZITO1950®`: `TeX Gyre Adventor` regular 400, +.02em tracking (`font-brand`), an Avant-Garde-style geometric sans. Regular and bold 700 are self-hosted as Latin WOFF2 subsets under `src/assets/fonts/`, licensed under the GUST Font License; use regular for the logo’s thin-stroke character. Prose mentioning the brand keeps its surrounding typeface.
- Scale: display `clamp(2.5rem,5vw,4.5rem)`/1.08 · h2 `clamp(2rem,3.6vw,3rem)`/1.15 · h3 1.5rem/1.25 · body 1.0625rem/1.7 (≤60ch) · small .875rem/1.5 · eyebrow .75rem uppercase +.18em (max one per three sections).
- Headings `text-wrap: balance`, paragraphs `pretty`. The text inscriptions use the brand font; the logo artwork remains `logo.svg` (Header/Footer), never reconstructed as text.

## Layout
- Container 1240px, gutter `clamp(1.25rem,4vw,4rem)`, section padding `clamp(3.5rem,8vw,8rem)`.
- Spacing scale 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96, 128px.
- 12-column editorial grid on desktop; below 768px one column, natural reading order, no horizontal overflow.
- Sections flow on the white page, separated by a 1px `line` hairline top border; no card shells. Photos radius 1.25rem (the hero photo 2rem); no shadows, gradients, glass or grain. Buttons and the three links under the hero are fully rounded. Line icons only on functional controls (cart, the links under the hero, footer social links).
- Sticky header 72px; anchors use `scroll-margin-top: 72px`.
- Section order: Hero → three links (Collezione limitata `#edizioni-limitate`, La nostra storia `#storia`, Perché Zito? `#perche-zito`) → Orologi (`#orologi`) → Storia (`#storia`) → Profumi (`#profumi`) → Officina (`#officina`) → Territorio (`#territorio`) → Perché Zito (`#perche-zito`) → Dove siamo (`#contatti`). Collection and history links follow the same order as their page sections.
- Expandable sections (`Panel`): a short preview is always visible. Split previews (including Territorio and Perché Zito) put the shared red-outline, uppercase arrow button directly under the introductory text, as in Officina; ordinary previews centre the button below. Expanded content is unchanged, with “Mostra meno” centred at the bottom. Closing preserves the button's screen position; body is `hidden` while closed. Links into a closed body open it, update the hash and scroll once after rendering.
- Split sections (Storia, Officina): photo on the left, text column on the right (vertically centred) holding the brand-red eyebrow, the serif h2 (max 14ch, balanced), the copy and the closed toggle; stacked on mobile with the photo on top. Officina splits 7/12 photo, 5/12 text. Storia uses a two-column 5fr/7fr grid (gap 4rem, 6rem from `lg`; a two-track grid so the gap is applied once) and shows the portrait archive poster (`new_history_photo.jpeg`, 533×800) whole, capped at 460px so it is never upscaled far, with the same 1.25rem rounded corners and muted caption below as the other photos. The split toggle is a fully rounded pill with a 1px `brand` (#d42e2e, the logo red) outline and `brand` uppercase tracked text plus “→”, filling `brand` with white text on hover; when open, the “Mostra meno” toggle sits centred under the body.
- Perfume photos share one square 856×856 canvas, cropped to the products with the same window, so the bottle and the box keep their relative size and baseline with no empty band above them.
- Orologi “Catalogo completo”: after opening, the page scrolls instantly so the catalogue's second row sits just under the sticky header.

## Components
- Header: white sticky bar, `logo.svg` left, only two navigation links (Orologi uomo `#orologi-uomo`, Orologi donna `#orologi-donna`) right as `.nav-pill` buttons: fully rounded, 44px, 1px `line` hairline, small sans ink-2 text, never filled; a pill is current (`aria-current`, bolder 2px ink outline, also in the mobile menu) while the catalogue is open and filtered to exactly its line, and stops being current when the filter changes or the catalogue closes. Each opens the full catalogue with its line filter, including unisex watches. History stays on the page and is reachable through the hero links and footer, not the header menu. Functional outline-SVG shopping-bag button opens Cart, square ink count badge when non-empty; no floating cart button; hairline after scroll; mobile menu as modal with focus trap.
- Hero: the admin-configurable `catalog.hero` photo (independent of every product photo; changed only in admin «Foto principale») is itself a link to `#orologi` (image scales ≤1.02 on hover), clipped to a 2rem radius; on desktop its slot reserves `min(56vh,32rem)`, on mobile it hugs the photo (a 14rem placeholder only while loading); it stays empty until the server catalogue arrives, so the built-in photo never flashes. Beside it h1 “ZITO1950”, the motto “Officine del tempo” in small uppercase tracking, a short ink rule, the serif lead “Dal 1950, una storia italiana.”, short text and one rounded ink button “Scopri la collezione” (`#orologi`).
- SectionButtons: three links under the hero in a hairline-divided row (stacked on mobile), ordered collection → history → why ZITO; each uses the same outlined triquetra icon, a serif uppercase label and a muted one-line hint.
- Orologi: closed shows 3–4 watches from admin `catalog.featured` (fallback: first 4 of the featured sort); “Catalogo completo” opens filters and the full grid; exclude `hidden`; featured sort puts `isNew` first, then `buy`, `no-buy` and `store-only` together, `out-of-stock`, price descending within each group.
- Officina (Assistenza): owner photo `img/storia/owner.jpg` beside the eyebrow “Assistenza ZITO1950®”, title “La cura del tempo, nel tempo.” and the first paragraph; “Scopri l’assistenza” opens the services, how to request assistance and the signature.
- Section bodies (Officina, Territorio, Perché Zito) share `SectionText` across the full container width, never a narrow column: headed blocks put the serif h3 in the left 4/12 and the paragraphs in the right 8/12 (two text columns from `lg` when a block has more than two paragraphs); paragraphs without a heading start at the photo's left edge (the container's left edge for Officina and Perché, column 6 for Territorio); titled points form a hairline-topped grid like the former Officina notes (serif h3 title, small ink-2 text; 1 column on mobile, 2 from `sm`, up to 5 in one row from `lg`, otherwise rows of 4); the signature sits on a hairline row, small uppercase tracked brand line left, serif h3 sentence right. Text verbatim from the client; brand written `ZITO1950®`.
- Brand: always `ZITO1950®` (uppercase, no space between ZITO and 1950, ® right after 1950); in the display-size hero h1 the ® is a 0.32em superscript. Surnames (Luigi Zito…) and verbatim quotes (the handwritten «a Zito 1950» dedication) stay as written.
- Catalog search: `type=search` pill (rounded-full hairline, 44px, magnifier icon) before the filters button, full width on mobile and 18rem from `sm`; live, accent/punctuation-insensitive match on the model name; Esc and “Azzera filtri” clear it.
- Filters: outlined “Filtri e ordinamento” button with ink count badge, result count, “Azzera filtri” and removable outlined chips. Native `<dialog>` side sheet, 420px from the right on desktop, full screen on mobile; first the Linea group (Uomo, Donna; unisex models match both), then sort radios, then checkbox groups (Selezione, Disponibilità, Movimento, Funzioni, Cassa, Prezzo) with tabular live counts; zero-count options muted and disabled. `#edizioni-limitate` applies only the limited-edition filter.
- ModelCard: image and detail controls open ModelDialog at the selected variant/photo; product `object-fit: contain`, never cropping bezels/bracelets; name serif h3, codes small, price sans. Purchase control only for `availability: buy`; `store-only` (admin “Solo in negozio”) shows an outlined “Solo in negozio” tag with the other labels and a rounded “Contatti” button to `#contatti` in the Acquista slot (in ModelDialog it closes the dialog first), keeping the price; the server refuses to sell it.
- Badge/labels: “1 of 1” (admin flag `oneOfOne`, gold), “Nuovo” and “Edizione limitata” stacked at the image’s top-left corner, white ground, ink uppercase small type with tracking, 1px ink border, radius 0, smaller on cards so they never cover the watch; in ModelDialog they sit in a row under the price. “Esaurito” status tag outlined. A 1 of 1 watch that is not sold out shows “Unico pezzo disponibile” under the price. The remaining-pieces count (`piecesRemaining`) is admin-only and never reaches the public catalogue; at 0 pieces, and after a 1 of 1 sells, the watch stays listed as “Esaurito” and cannot be bought.
- Product video: watch and variant galleries may include videos (`.mp4` in `catalog.videos`). Only ModelDialog plays them: muted, inline, looped, autoplay when selected, with a round pause/play button and a timeline below; the thumbnail carries a play mark. Watch cards, hero, cart and emails use the first photo; perfume cards browse photos only.
- ModelDialog: native `<dialog>`; image views, name, codes, price, labels (plus an outlined Uomo / Donna / Unisex tag, sr-only “Linea:”), “Unico pezzo disponibile” on an unsold 1 of 1, quote, description, specs list; close button, Esc, backdrop; focus returns to opener; body scroll locked.
- Variant colours: admin “Due colori” enables an optional “Secondo colore” picker; disabling it retains the first colour. Existing `swatch` stores one or two hex colours, shared across translations. Product selectors show the two colours as equal diagonal halves with a sharp seam (the hard-stop gradient is a colour sample, not a decorative gradient); variant names remain the accessible labels.
- Recommended: “Scopri anche” inside ModelDialog, every other public model in one horizontal row: owner’s `similar` picks first, then by similarity. Square outlined prev/next buttons page by whole cards; wheel scrolls the row and releases at its ends. Selection replaces the current model in the open dialog, resets variant/photo, scrolls to top and focuses its title.
- Profumi: public perfumes form a two-column grid from 640px, stacked on mobile. Each card has its own square photo gallery (watch-style previous/next arrows, dots and touch swipe; controls absent for one photo), name, optional line, code/price, introduction, expandable description/quote/ingredients, “Mostra tutto” / “Mostra meno”, purchase/contact action, then plain-text specs. Blank paragraphs are ignored when selecting the introduction. Expanded text stays above both the toggle and purchase action; no shared details area below the cards. The toggle exposes `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`, and closing preserves its screen position. Specs remain small ink-2 wrapping text without pill outlines. Availability rules are unchanged: `buy` offers Acquista, `no-buy` requests information, `coming-soon` shows all description without price/code/quote/action/toggle, `out-of-stock` shows Esaurito with muted price and no purchase action, `hidden` is omitted.
- Storia: `#storia`, archive photo `img/storia/storia-nuova.jpg` beside the 1951 railway intro, cropped (`object-cover`) to `min(26rem,55vh)` tall on desktop and 60vw on mobile so the closed section stays compact; “Scopri la storia” opens every chronological chapter and the closing quote; chapter images alternate sides on desktop.
- Territorio: `#territorio`, photo remains on the right on desktop and first on mobile. The text column holds its red eyebrow, balanced serif title “Dove la bellezza incontra il tempo”, the italic serif lead, the two opening lines and the shared red-outline arrow button. The expanded body holds the headed blocks and the signature.
- Perché Zito: `#perche-zito`, photo remains on the left on desktop and first on mobile. Its text column holds the balanced serif title “Perché ZITO1950®”, italic lead, first paragraph and shared red-outline arrow button. The expanded body holds the vision text, the seven titled values, the closing block and the signature.
  - Closed buttons use section-specific copy: “Scopri l’assistenza”, “Scopri Scalea” and “Cosa ci distingue”, localized in all site languages; the existing “Mostra meno” close action is unchanged.
- Contatti: “Dove siamo” at `#contatti`; map centred (max 56rem, 4:3 on mobile, 16:9 from `sm`), directly under it a Google rating pill (hairline, fully rounded, 44px: monochrome ink “G”, serif “5,0”, ★★★★★, muted underlined “31 recensioni su Google”), linking to the Google listing in a new tab; then email customer@zito1950.it as a `mailto:` row centred under it. No review texts on the site. No postal address, company or P.IVA in the section, footer or metadata; the address stays in data only as the map query.
- Cart: opened from Header; native `<dialog>` with watch and perfume lines, quantities, total and PayPal checkout. Each line has two collapsible optional sections: “Certificato di proprietà e garanzia” (certificate front/back thumbnails, Nome, Cognome, Email, all required once one is filled) and “Incisione” (textarea, n/10 parole counter, 120 chars; when filled a bordered notice states the item is personalized and not returnable, with a required acknowledgement checkbox). Personalized lines lock quantity to 1. “Paga con PayPal” stays disabled while a section is incomplete, and always in the GitHub Pages preview (with a note).
  - Case-back engraving: watches carry an admin flag `engravable` (“Incisione sul retro possibile”; never for sapphire, display or enamelled backs). Only engravable watches show the “Incisione sul retro” cart section, the ModelDialog note and the “Incisione sul retro” filter; the server rejects engraving on other watches. Perfumes keep their engraving; the certificate stays on every line.
  - Perfume suggestion: with a watch in the cart, show available-to-buy perfumes with a positive catalog price that are not already present on any perfume line. Photo, name and actual price accompany an explicit “Aggiungi al carrello” action; never auto-add. The cart stays open, watch personalization stays intact and the added suggestion disappears. Actions are disabled during payment or pending PayPal approval; suggestions are absent in empty/perfume-only carts, after payment or on catalog error.

Addition and total update immediately; the suggestion stays in place for a drawn checkmark, fades out after 540ms, then the new cart row enters with a 300ms fade/10px rise. Duplicate presses are ignored; keyboard focus moves to the added product. Reduced motion skips movement and the confirmation delay. If the watch disappears during feedback, the perfume line stays visible.

Decorative watermark centred horizontally and vertically in the footer on desktop and mobile, at 4% opacity; no input interception or accessible image name. Foreground remains ink/muted; worst-case muted text contrast over its black pixels is about 4.88:1.

## States
- Focus-visible: 2px ink outline, 3px offset, on every interactive element.
- Hover (fine pointers only): `.btn` inverts to ink fill; `.nav-pill` keeps its white ground and gets a bolder 2px ink outline (inset, no layout shift) with ink text and a 1px lift (300ms); `.link-arrow` underline darkens, arrow shifts 4px; cards: image scales ≤1.02.
- Active: 1px press; `.nav-pill` compresses to 0.96 (160ms). Buy and contact buttons (`.btn-motion`: Acquista, store-only Contatti, Richiedi informazioni) lift 2px on fine-pointer hover (200ms) and compress to 0.97 on press (120ms) instead of the 1px press. Reduced motion keeps the outline/fill change and drops lift and press. Disabled: checkout controls while busy or unavailable.
- Images: explicit width/height from `public/img/manifest.json`, WebP with JPG fallback, lazy below the fold.

## Motion
- Ease `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, 500ms. Reveal: opacity 0→1 + 8px rise, `.reveal` + `.is-visible` toggled by IntersectionObserver, once.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables reveals, smooth scroll and transitions.
- No parallax, loops, scroll-jacking, smooth-scroll libraries. The only looping motion is an admin-uploaded product video, muted, inside an open ModelDialog and pausable; with reduced motion it does not autoplay and waits for the play button.

## Prohibitions
Dark mode or dark sections; stock/generated imagery; invented facts, prices, dates, names, specs or testimonials; shadows; gradients; emoji; autoplay carousels.

## Content rules
- Italian source facts come from the catalogue, price card and old site; interface microcopy is separate. Unreadable = `[illeggibile]`. Localized copy must preserve the facts.
- Seed prices exactly as printed (e.g. `3.890 EUR`); published catalog prices come from the administrator.
- Source copy lives in `src/data/content.ts`, bundled translations in `src/data/translations.json`, editable product copy and prices in the catalog. Components hold no copy beyond UI labels.
